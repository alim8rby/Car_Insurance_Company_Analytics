Car Insurance Company Analytics

An interactive Power BI analytics project exploring customer behavior, policy preferences, sales-channel performance, financial metrics, and complaint patterns for a car insurance company.

The project transforms insurance customer data into business-focused insights through an analytical dashboard designed to support marketing, sales, policy, and customer-experience decisions.


Project Overview

The dashboard analyzes several dimensions of the insurance business:

Customer demographics: Customer Lifetime Value (CLV), gender, employment status, state, and income

Policy portfolio: Coverage, policy type, and number of policies

Sales & offers: Sales channel, renewal offers, and customer response

Claims & complaints: Total claim amount and open complaints

Vehicle characteristics: Vehicle class and vehicle size

Business Questions

The analysis is designed to answer questions such as:

Where are the company's customers concentrated geographically?

How do customer characteristics vary across regions and employment status?

Which policy types and coverage plans generate the highest subscription volume?

Which sales channels reach the largest customer segments?

How do customers respond to different offer types across sales channels?

Which vehicle classes and sizes are most represented in each sales channel?

How do complaints vary by policy type and date?

Which customer segments present potential opportunities for higher-value policies?

Where are sales and customer-experience improvements most needed?

Key Findings

According to the dashboard analysis:

Customer concentration: Most clients are employed, suburban, and located in California.

Policy preference: Personal Auto policies dominate the portfolio.

Coverage opportunity: Premium coverage is associated with higher-income customers.

Sales performance: Agent-based sales perform best, while web-based offers struggle.

Complaints: Overall complaint volume is relatively low, with complaints mainly associated with corporate policies.


Business Recommendations

Focus Marketing on Suburban Customers

Prioritize campaigns toward suburban customer segments, particularly in high-revenue states such as California.

Strengthen Premium Offerings

Use customer income and segment characteristics to better position Premium policies toward high-income customers, particularly in urban segments.

Optimize Web Sales

Improve online engagement through more targeted offers, incentives, and renewal strategies to address weaker web-based performance.

Monitor Corporate Policy Complaints

Investigate the sources of complaints associated with Corporate Auto policies and use the findings to improve the customer experience and reduce complaint rates.

Analytical Approach

The project follows a business-oriented analytics workflow:

Understand the business context

Identify relevant dimensions and measures

Explore customer, policy, sales, financial, and complaint patterns

Segment the customer and policy portfolio

Compare performance across states, regions, channels, and customer characteristics

Visualize relationships and trends in Power BI

Translate analytical findings into business recommendations


Project Outcome

This project demonstrates how raw insurance data can be transformed into an interactive business intelligence solution that connects:

Customer behavior → Policy preferences → Sales channels → Financial performance → Complaints → Business decisions

The objective is not only to visualize the data, but to turn the analysis into actionable insights for customer targeting, policy strategy, sales optimization, and customer-experience improvement.
